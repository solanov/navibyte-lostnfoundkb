Terms of Service & Operational Mandates

Last Updated: October 2023

By accessing The Academic Curator, you agree to abide by the institutional mandates set forth by the governing body. These operational guidelines ensure the integrity, security, and efficient management of all cataloged assets.

1. Thirty-Day Retention Lifecycle

All items logged within the system, both physical articles and digital records, are subject to a strict thirty-day retention lifecycle from the date of initial entry.

  • Automated notifications will be dispatched to claimant accounts 7 days prior to the expiration date.
  • Physical assets unclaimed post-retention will be marked for authorized institutional disposal or donation.
  • Digital records associated with expired items will be permanently purged from the active database to maintain archival efficiency.

2. Administrative Access

Access to the Curator Logs, Archive Management tools, and cross-departmental analytics is strictly restricted. Only authorized institutional personnel with verified credentials may execute structural changes to the database. Unauthorized attempts to modify entry statuses or access restricted archives will result in immediate suspension of account privileges and formal institutional review.
