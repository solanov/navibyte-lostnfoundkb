The Academic Curator

Institutional Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2023

1. Collection of Unredacted Asset Data

Under the guidelines set forth by the SECI Framework, The Academic Curator platform collects and processes asset data necessary for the identification and curation of academic property. Staff-only access is granted to specific data points, including "Hidden Notes" and unredacted images, to ensure rigorous verification standards are met.

This unredacted access is strictly limited to authorized curatorial staff and is governed by strict institutional audit trails to prevent unauthorized external access or data exfiltration.

2. Data Retention and Archival Standards

In alignment with long-term academic research mandates, the platform implements a rolling 36-month log retention policy for all transactional and operational data regarding asset claims and audits.

Key academic assets and related curatorial metadata are subject to indefinite archival for institutional research purposes. This long-term archival is critical for historical analysis and the continuous refinement of the university's academic asset taxonomy.
